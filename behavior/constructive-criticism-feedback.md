# Interview Question: What is the last constructive criticism you received?

## Question:
**What is the last constructive criticism you received?**

## Answer:
I was told I appeared inconsistent for pushing code on a release day, despite setting a rule against it for the team. In that case, I made a judgment call because it was a critical fix and we still had time before release. I also noticed that even after setting the rule, not everyone was following it consistently, which made enforcement more complex.

The feedback helped me realize the importance of leading with clarity and communicating exceptions transparently. Since then, I’ve been more deliberate about aligning the team around shared ownership and reinforcing consistency — not just through rules, but through example.
